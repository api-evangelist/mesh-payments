Introduction Managing corporate travel is no small task—especially when expenses, bookings, and policy compliance are spread across teams and tools. That’s where effective business travel reporting comes in. For finance, operations, and travel managers, clear and consistent travel data is essential for controlling costs, enforcing policies, and making smarter business decisions.
